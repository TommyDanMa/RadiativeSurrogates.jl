# Summary

`RadiativeSurrogates.jl` builds and, more importantly, *diagnoses* reduced-basis
neural surrogates for the heat equation with a strongly nonlinear
Stefan–Boltzmann radiation boundary condition ($u^4$ heat rejection to a 3 K
deep-space sink), the regime of spacecraft radiator design. The division of
labour is strict: Gridap assembles finite element residuals and Jacobians
[@Badia2020]; the SciML stack solves them, NonlinearSolve for steady Newton
problems [@Pal2024] and OrdinaryDiffEq mass-matrix integrators for transients
[@Rackauckas2017]. Surrogates follow the non-intrusive reduced-basis recipe
[@Quarteroni2016]: a POD basis from snapshots plus a small neural network
mapping parameters to basis coefficients, sidestepping the hyper-reduction
(DEIM [@Chaturantabut2010]) that an intrusive treatment of the $u^4$ term
would require.

The package's distinctive content is the diagnostic pipeline around the
surrogate: measurement of the solution-manifold spectrum with grid- and
element-independence checks; structural rank experiments pre-registered in
script headers before first run, with misses reported as misses; exact
reduced-Galerkin error floors that split surrogate error into basis error and
coefficient-map error; and verification artifacts rarely shipped with
surrogates — grid-convergence (GCI) error bands on the full-order quantities
of interest, N-seed training ensembles, a measured out-of-distribution guard,
a floating-point precision audit, and forward uncertainty propagation checked
against an exact full-order Monte Carlo reference. Every number in the
accompanying report is regenerated by numbered deterministic scripts
(StableRNGs + Sobol sampling) and reprinted from frozen artifacts by a single
diff-able printer script.

# Statement of need

Non-intrusive reduced-order modeling is widely used, but the *decision* it
rests on — whether the solution manifold is intrinsically low-dimensional, so
that learning a handful of coefficients is the right factorization — is
usually assumed rather than tested. `RadiativeSurrogates.jl` provides a
compact, fully reproducible testbed in which that decision is made
explicitly, on a problem with genuine boundary nonlinearity, and in which the
two possible answers both occur: the steady manifold collapses to numerical
rank 3 (coefficient learning is provably map-limited, not basis-limited),
while load-switching transients re-thicken the manifold and move the
bottleneck to the data. Researchers get a verified, laptop-scale nonlinear
benchmark for methods work (latent dynamics in the stiff, load-switching
regime [@Kim2021], operator-learning baselines, hyper-reduction); educators get a worked example of pre-registered
computational science with an honest verification tier table; practitioners
get a template for what a surrogate should ship with before it is trusted:
error floors, validity guards, discretization error bands, and seed spread.

# Functionality

- Full-order model: Gridap `CartesianDiscreteModel` with P1/P2 Lagrangian
  elements, two boundary configurations, manufactured-solution hooks through
  the nonlinear boundary condition, and an exact discrete energy balance.
- Steady solves: NonlinearSolve Newton with sparse Jacobian prototypes,
  analytic radiative-equilibrium initial guesses, and warm starts along
  parameter sweeps.
- Transient solves: mass-matrix method of lines with ImplicitEuler/Trapezoid
  and adaptive FBDF; square-wave load cycles hit exactly via `tstops`.
- Reduction and learning: POD/SVD compression, whitened-coefficient MLP and
  Kolmogorov–Arnold [@Liu2024] mappers, direct field-regression baseline, and
  space-time mappers with phase-harmonic features.
- Metrics: relative $L^2$, coefficient error, the assembled nonlinear PDE
  residual of any prediction, and design quantities of interest (radiated
  power, peak temperature).
- Reproduction: numbered scripts `00–27` regenerate every artifact, figure,
  and table; an interactive GLMakie console solves the full-order model live
  against the surrogate.

# Quality control

A 68-assertion test suite runs in CI: manufactured-solution convergence at
the theoretical order through the nonlinear boundary condition, automatic
differentiation versus finite-difference versus hand-written Jacobians, exact
discrete energy balance, θ-family time-integration orders, a nodally exact 1D
radiative equilibrium, POD round-trips, and skip-guarded regression tripwires
that pin the deployed surrogates' quality with 3× margin whenever the data
artifacts are present.
